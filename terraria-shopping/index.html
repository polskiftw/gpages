Terraria Shopping

A searchable acquisition planner for compound crafting projects. Fishing routes are separated from the ingredients you still have to buy, find, mine, or murder something for.

How this planner defines “fishable”: the base ingredient has a practical route through direct fishing, fishing crates/lock boxes, or an Angler quest reward. Shimmer conversions from a fishable item also count. Lock-box routes still require the appropriate Dungeon/Shadow key.

Project recipes use leaf ingredients rather than intermediate crafts, so you can shop once and combine everything later. Where a recipe has multiple equivalent routes, this planner chooses a clean canonical route and notes the alternatives.
