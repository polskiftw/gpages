Terraria Shopping

Searchable crafting projects with fishing routes, remaining sources, and explicit availability gates so “after a boss” never gets confused with “Hardmode-only.”

Reading availability: every item and project has two separate labels. Pre-Hardmode / Hardmode tells you which world mode is required. The second label tells you the earliest actual gate or condition — boss, NPC rescue, event, moon phase, fishing method, etc.
PRE-HARDMODEAfter Skeletron • Dungeon
means you can get it before Wall of Flesh, but not before Skeletron. A Golem drop is shown as HARDMODE After Plantera • Golem drop, because Golem itself is Hardmode and Plantera-gated; it does not say “post-Golem,” since the first Golem kill can give you the item.

Fishable means the base ingredient has a practical route through direct fishing, crates/lock boxes, an Angler reward, or a Shimmer conversion originating from a fishable item. Lock-box routes can still require Dungeon/Shadow keys.
