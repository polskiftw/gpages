Terraria Shopping

Every terminal craftable as a project, every craftable as a searchable component, and the hand-verified acquisition routes we actually care about layered on top.

Projects are craftable results that are not ingredients in another normal crafting recipe — the actual ends of the recipe graph. Components exposes every craftable result, including intermediates.

Complexity counts distinct dependencies reachable through the full recipe graph, not raw stack quantity. That keeps “make 999 bricks” from outranking genuinely gnarly projects just because it consumes a large pile. Open a card for a flattened shopping list, recipe options, and an expandable canonical recipe path.

Generated shopping lists choose a cycle-safe canonical normal crafting route. Hand-verified projects such as Shellphone and Ankh Shield override the generated leaf list where a human route is more useful. Fishable and Remaining are the curated acquisition-source layer; unannotated generated ingredients are never guessed.
