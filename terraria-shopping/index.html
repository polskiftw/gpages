Terraria Shopping

Pick something to craft, see the unique items you still need, and check them off as you collect them.

Projects are finished craftables — items that are not used as an ingredient in another normal recipe. Components includes every craftable item, including intermediates.

The default sort is unique shopping-list items needed. Quantity does not affect the ranking: 9,000 of one ingredient still counts as one unique item.

Each card tracks fishing-route items separately from the non-fishable remainders. Fishing routes include direct catches, fishing crates and their contents, Angler quest rewards, and loot from fishing-spawned enemies. If none of the required items are fishable, the card says LANDLOCKED instead.

Acquisition badges use color by method: blue for fishing, red for killing an enemy or boss, and gold for vendor purchases. Purchase badges show the seller and base shop price.

Open a card for the shopping list and its direct crafting recipe. Corruption/Crimson substitutions are grouped together, so swapping an equivalent world-evil ingredient does not pretend to be a separate recipe.
