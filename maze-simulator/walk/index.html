Desktop: Click the maze to lock your mouse. WASD to move, move mouse to look, Shift to run, Escape to release cursor. Phone: Drag the view to look and hold the onscreen buttons to walk. The mouse wheel and touch gestures do not change the actual maze.

Built directly from the Connected Detours 3D graph. Geometry streams in local view regions; the underlying passages never depend on render chunks. Surfaces use four small repeated procedural patterns (brick, floor stone, ceiling stone, and stairs). No prefab staircases or imported 3D models. This is the first playable geometry/collision prototype, not a finished game.
