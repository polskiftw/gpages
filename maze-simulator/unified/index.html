What this milestone proves

The generator reuses Connected Detours for every flat floor and adds only a small number of reciprocal Euclidean ramps between them. Some stairs continue through an otherwise solid intermediate floor tile, so a climb can pass multiple floors without becoming a landing. No fixed staircase patterns, and no generation chunks.

Everything derives from one connected navigation graph. We test exact preservation of the original horizontal walls, reachability, vertical clearance on overlapping passage segments, stair rarity and seed reproduction. This remains a math preview, not the final 3D wall, ceiling or collision-mesh renderer.
