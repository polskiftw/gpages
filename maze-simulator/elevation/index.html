Stairs are passages.

The generator chooses flat or rising/falling connections one step at a time. Multiple climbs can continue straight through floor elevations without stopping. Turns, junctions, and new flights can emerge when the math makes a landing. No L-, U-, or spiral-stair prefab templates.

Actual physical scale

Every horizontal step advances 7 m. A climbing step gains or loses 3.5 m, so every rising segment has the same realistic 26.6° incline. The walkable ribbon is 1.5 m wide, with 2.1 m headroom and a 0.25 m floor allowance. A physical-space reservation test prevents intersecting overlapping passage ribbons. Cells on different levels may occupy the same X/Y footprint if they have vertical clearance.

This is a new elevation-aware topology prototype. The approved Connected Detours generator stays available on the main page while we test the new movement mathematics. Here, every stair link is horizontally displaced, unlike the older abstract same-coordinate U/D links. A complete 3D renderer and staircase/ceiling meshes have not yet been built.

Each SVG and JSON edge comes directly from stairs.mjs. No server or game engine. Generation is global and deterministic; there are no generation chunks or stair design templates.
